Athan recordings bundled with سعودي TV (the screensaver app's «الأذان»)
=========================================================================

Each file was trimmed, mixed down to mono, loudness-normalised (-16 LUFS) and encoded to Opus/OGG at 48 kbps (the
islamweb.net three and athan-hayya.ogg at 64 kbps) by this project (optimize/run is where the originals were downloaded;
they are not part of the repository). Nothing else was changed (the "Changes" lines say where each was cut). The settings screen shows
these same credits («الأذان» › «حقوق التسجيلات»).

The default athan (Fajr included) is athan-hayya.ogg, a private recording supplied by the project's owner, not under
a free licence. The next three are islamweb.net's recordings (إسلام ويب, audio.islamweb.net), not under a free licence
either: they are bundled by the owner's decision, with credit to islamweb.net and a link to each recording's page.
