Athan recordings bundled with سعودي TV (the screensaver app's «الأذان»)
=========================================================================

Each file was trimmed, mixed down to mono, loudness-normalised (-16 LUFS) and encoded to Opus/OGG at 48 kbps by
this project (optimize/run is where the originals were downloaded; they are not part of the repository). Nothing
else was changed (the "Changes" lines say where each was cut). The settings screen shows these same credits («الأذان» › «حقوق التسجيلات»).
